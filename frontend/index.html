Before we start

PathWay helps you explore UK career options, review AI-generated role suggestions, strengthen job applications, and practise interviews for any professional background. Suggestions are not live vacancies.

  • Your CV and answers are processed to generate personalized career guidance.
  • We do not keep a database. Data lives in this browser tab and in server memory until restart.
  • This is not legal, immigration, or medical advice. Verify with official sources and a human adviser.
